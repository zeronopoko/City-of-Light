# The City: Basics

## Geography
- One huge urban mass, roughly the size of a small country (about 227 x 220 km) with around 7 billion people.
- South: the Great Lake. Other sides: the Outskirts (wasteland beyond the walls). Underneath: the Ruins.
- 26 Districts, each run by one Wing named by letter (A Corp = District 1 ... Z Corp = District 26). Maps only show 25. Z Corp is confirmed to exist, but its location is unknown.
- Four quarters: North, East, South, West. Culture, climate and clothing follow them.
  - North: cold, heavy loose clothes, lots of decoration.
  - East: Chinese-inspired, cloth-based.
  - South: modern Western formal (T Corp is steampunk).
  - West: leather and metal armor, medieval feel.
- Districts 1-3 (A, B, C) sit at the center and belong to the Head.

## The Head
- The supreme power of the City. Probably a group, identity unknown.
- A Corp (District 1): patents, minting Ahn, authorizing Wings. B Corp = the Eye (surveillance, tax evaders). C Corp = the Claw (executioners).
- Rarely meddles in Wings' local affairs. It sets City-wide Taboos (see 04) and can declare something an Impurity, to be destroyed or expelled to the Outskirts.
- Its idea of the City: humans only. Sapient non-humans and human-like machines were driven out long ago.

## Wings
- 26 conglomerates that govern a District each. A corporation becomes a Wing by owning and using a Singularity (see 03).
- Employees are called feathers. Being a feather is the most secure life available.
- A Wing protects and rules its Nest. It mostly ignores or exploits its Backstreets.
- Wing laws are Taboos, enforced by Taboo Hunters (Fixers on contract). Lethal force without warning is allowed.
- If a Wing falls: its Singularity patent expires, the Nest decays into a slum, Syndicates fight over the land, and a new corporation may rise to replace it.

## Nests and Backstreets
**Nest**
- Safe, clean, expensive. High taxes, overtime, constant fear of losing your job.
- Entry through checkpoints. Work/tourist visas for visiting. A migration permit to live there is extremely expensive and sought after.
- Backstreets residents are kept out by plasma fields, glass walls or plain fences. Nobody crosses without permission.

**Backstreets**
- Slums with twisted roads, run by Syndicates, Offices and neighborhood watches. Residential areas are the safest parts.
- Most people live here. Many study in "examinee towns" to pass Wing exams and move up.
- Syndicates and Fixers have their bases here. The Five Fingers rule it and even the Head struggles to reach in.

**Night in the Backstreets**
- About 3:14 to 4:34 AM (80 minutes; some sources say 3:13). Nearly everything is permitted.
- Only two rules: do not violate residential buildings, and do not record or investigate what happens. Eyewitness testimony is allowed.
- Sweepers sweep the streets in waves and clear everything outside residences, including corpses and loose items.
- Nest citizens are not covered by these rules, but can be dragged out into the Backstreets.

## Who does what
- **Fixers**: licensed mercenaries/handymen graded 9 to 1. Work through Offices and Associations. Details in 02.
- **Syndicates**: unlicensed organized groups, from tiny gangs to the Five Fingers (Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky), who rival Wings.
- **Workshops**: make weapons and gear under patent. **Tailors** make special clothing that works as armor.

## Money and daily life
- Currency: Ahn. About 4 million a month is upper middle class. Most people cannot save.
- District 20 uses time as currency.
- Money solves nearly everything. Disease is curable on paper, but only if you can pay.
- Death is common and mass casualties are shrugged off unless they happen inside a Nest. Funerals are rare. People avoid close bonds and replace lost ones quickly.
- Apathy is widespread. Carmen called it the "disease of the mind". People tie themselves to a group (Wing, Finger, Office) for safety and meaning.
- No major organized religion. Cults exist, and the Index's Prescripts act like a "god".
- 13 common languages. Names come from many cultures, are not tied to gender, and surnames are rare.
- No racial or gender discrimination. Class discrimination is extreme.
- Not everything is networked: phones, pagers, radios, TV and newspapers exist, but some areas still rely on letters.

## Threat levels
Hazards are graded by how much people will pay to remove them (not by raw danger):

Urban Myth < Urban Legend < Urban Plague < Urban Nightmare < Star of the City < Impurity

Hana Association grades everything up to Star of the City. Only the Head declares Impurities.

## Beyond the walls
- **Outskirts**: junkyard of the City. Small settlements, abandoned children, Sweeper-type scavengers, many monsters (some sentient, some man-made). Things the Head rejects end up here.
- **Ruins**: deep underground, dangerous, hold rare Relics.
- **Great Lake**: divided into sections ("Lakes") with their own laws. Managed by U Corp. Whales and Mermaids live here.
- Beyond the Outskirts are stronger monsters, which is why almost nobody explores.

## Distortion and E.G.O.
- After the White Nights and Dark Days (the fall of Lobotomy Corporation), people under extreme emotional collapse may hear a warm woman's voice (Carmen).
- Accepting it: **Distortion**, a monster form built on the person's desire or pain. Rejecting it and keeping control: **E.G.O.**, a personal weapon or armor. Failing to do either: **Peccatulum**.
- Distortions can sometimes be talked or fought back to human. Left alone long enough, they become Abnormalities (permanent).
- Monoliths (black steles) can force people to Distort.
- Rare: starting characters should not have E.G.O. (see 02).

## History in short
- The City is at least 400 years old.
- The Smoke War (a Wing War) ended around year 974. Lobotomy Corporation helped topple the old L Corp and took its place.
- Lobotomy Corporation's project ended in the White Nights and Dark Days. The Library then rose in L Corp's former Nest and was later exiled to the Outskirts with District 12's Nest.
- Main story timeline starts January 984.

## Unknown or unconfirmed (do not invent)

- Location of District 26.
- Many Wings' Singularities and inner workings (E, O, Y, Z, others).
- What Eight Association does.
- Whether there is a 12th named Fixer Association (some sources say 13).
