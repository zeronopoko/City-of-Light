# Taboos and Laws

A **Taboo** is a law for a territory. There are two levels.

## Two levels
| Level | Set by | Covers | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| City Taboo | The Head | Everyone, everywhere | Effectively a death sentence. The Claw executes, Sweepers clean up. |
| Nest Taboo | A Wing | Its whole District (Nest and Backstreets) | Taboo Hunters are sent after the violator. Lethal force without warning is allowed. |

Even the Five Fingers avoid breaking City Taboos. Taboos in Nests are sometimes broken by strong people who can dodge the hunters.

## City Taboos (the Head)
- Fully resurrecting the dead (mind and body together). Limited methods like H Corp life insurance (brain revival) are allowed.
- AI Ethics Amendment:
  - No machines or bodies that look like humans.
  - No machines with human-level mind or emotion.
  - No sapient non-human beings in the City.
- Cloning: a clone and the original may not exist together for more than 7 days (A Corp's time). A Wing that breaks this can be shut down.
- Violating residential areas during the Night in the Backstreets.
- Recording or investigating the Night in the Backstreets (eyewitness testimony excepted).
- Not paying taxes: three warnings, then death after the fourth missed payment.
- Weapon rules:
  - Larger caliber means shorter barrel.
  - No gun may pierce steel or building walls.
  - Gunfire must be audible.
  - Do not research or imagine guns that break the guidelines.
  - Only authorized Workshops may make S-class firearms.
  - Explosives must be planted, not thrown, and with a limited radius.
- Patent infringement (Workshops): up to three cease-and-desist letters, all profit handed to A Corp, then the Claw comes.

## Known Nest Taboos
- **K Corp (District 11)**: disturbing the Nest immigration checkpoint (Taboo K185). Breaking the glass wall triggers a code purple response.
- **N Corp (District 14)**: creating or even viewing recorded footage that looks real. Even an accidental glance counts. Low-quality footage is more tolerated.
- **U Corp (District 21)**: recording the Laws of the Great Lake, even by tattooing them.
- Other Wings have their own. Check the district file before assuming.

## Taboo Hunters
- Fixers hired by a Wing (or other authority) to catch violators. Usually few in number.
- Sent by Grade depending on the seriousness. Repeat offenders get stronger hunters.
- Priority: stop the violation (destroy or retrieve the footage or item). Capture comes next. N Corp's hunters take people alive to the Wing, and those taken rarely come back.
- Can chase across Districts only with the other Wing's authorization.
- Hunters can also chase contract breakers (such as runaway Butlers).

## Fixer rules
- Every Fixer needs a Hana license. Every job is reported to Hana afterward.
- Fixers may advertise but may not threaten people into giving them work.
- Fixers should not kill unwilling civilians. Breaking this can mean demotion.
- Hana can add penalty points, suspend licenses and demote grades.
- Associate Fixers may not work with Syndicates (risk of death or demotion).
- Offices must draw up a deed of contract for jobs.
- No storing people in dimensional containers.

## Night in the Backstreets (summary)
- About 3:14 to 4:34 AM. Anything goes, except violating homes and recording.
- Sweepers clear everything outside residences.
- The Head's Claws punish anyone investigating the night.
- Violating a residence is a City Taboo. Everything else is "free".

## DM notes
- Always remind the player when they are about to break a Taboo and which level it is.
- City Taboos should feel overwhelming. The reaction is cold and efficient, not dramatic.
- Nest Taboos can be bargained around with money, allies or speed.
- Do not invent Taboos for real Wings that are not in the sources unless the player is fine with homebrew. Label homebrew clearly.
