## Notes
- Fallen Wings: Lobotomy Corporation (District 12), Old L Corp, Old G Corp.
- Aspirant Wings: Limbus Company and Wayfarer Company (not tied to a District).
- Regions: North is cold and ornate, East is Chinese-inspired, South is modern Western formal, West is leather and armor.
