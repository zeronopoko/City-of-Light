# Technology of the City

The City has every tech level at once: pagers beside teleporting trains. Quality scales with price. Nest citizens get safe versions; the Backstreets get cheap ones with side effects.

## Body modification
- **Augmentation**: boosts strength, speed, heart, reflexes. It lets the body handle combat; it does not teach technique.
- **Tattoo augmentation**: skin as hard as steel and extra strength. Once patented (a fallen Wing's Singularity), now cheap. Famous with the Middle.
- **Prosthetic substitution**: replace parts or the whole body except the brain. Irreversible for full-body. Needs brain fluid, fuel/electricity, maintenance. Cheap models lose senses; expensive ones adjust or shut off emotion.
- Styles: current full-body models look like mechanical humans with odd heads. Old square-box bodies are outdated. Bio-prosthetics (insect arms etc.) are out of style.
- Because of the Head's ban on copying bodies, human-looking robots are illegal, so prosthetic users stand out.

## Medicine and revival
- Nearly all disease is curable, but only for people who can pay.
- **K Corp**: HP / ampules, bioengineered regeneration. Constant supply makes Nest users very hard to kill. Decay ampules melt flesh.
- **H Corp**: Boluses (many kinds, with side effects) and life insurance that revives a person into a new body if the brain survives.
- **W Corp**: restoration of a person to a recorded state.
- **Memory wipe**: standard (removes a memory cleanly) and cheap Backstreets version (destroys it, may damage other memories).

## Clothing and gear
- **Fabric**: Tailor-made clothing with protection, speed or emotional effects (courage). Some made from living beings via a fallen Wing's Singularity (the Carnival).
- **Workshops**: sell weapons and tools under patent. Examples: acceleration gloves, weight spheres, pressure bandages, prosthetic weapons.
- **Dimensional containers**: cases that store more than their size and reduce weight. Humans should not enter them (Hana and Devyat' ban it).

## Space, travel and communication
- **WARP Trains** (W Corp): reach anywhere in the City in 10 seconds. Passengers actually spend a very long time in an extradimensional space with no time passing.
  - First class: hibernation capsules, very expensive (a Backstreets resident's year of income).
  - Economy: cheaper but dangerous; passengers can go insane or violent. W Corp sends Cleanup Agents.
- **Other transport**: buses, normal trains, cars, subways. Hongyuan (H Corp) uses palanquins carried by people. Airplanes are not used.
- **Communication**: phones, pagers, radios, TV, newspapers. Computers are not common in daily life but a network exists. T Corp's District uses letters.
- **Corridors and Backdoors**: connect doors across Nests and the Outskirts. Dangerous without preparation. The Ring has long used them. Limbus Company's bus has one.
- **Mirror and glass technology**: from the League of Nine; shows other possibilities. Spreading across the City.

## Dangerous tech
- **Concept incinerator**: erases an invention or concept from the world, including records and memories (strength depends on price). A single remaining memory can bring it back.
- **Monolith**: black stele about two meters tall, can force Distortion or Peccatulum.
- **Golden Bough**: leftover essence of Lobotomy Corporation's energy tech; amplifies emotion and draws factions.
- **Enkephalin**: positive form is energy source; also a drug when abused. Made from Abnormalities.

## Singularities
A Singularity is a unique patented technology that breaks physics. A Wing must turn it into products to make money. When a Wing falls, its Singularity becomes public. Wings can buy Singularities from fallen Wings.
