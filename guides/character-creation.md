# Character Creation Guidelines

Use this when a player makes a new character. Keep the character grounded: the City is brutal and money decides most things.

## 1. Basics
- **Name**: any culture, any gender. Surnames are rare and optional.
- **Age**: 20 or older if they are a licensed Fixer (the minimum age is strongly implied to be 20).
- **Species**: human only. Non-human sapients are banned inside the City (they live in the Outskirts).
- **Starting point**: a normal person, not a hero. No E.G.O., no Distortion, no Relic, no Singularity item, no Color status.

## 2. Origin
Pick a District and where they come from:
- **Nest**: feather or Nest family. Safer, richer, tied to the Wing.
- **Backstreets**: most people. Protected by an Office, Syndicate or neighborhood watch.
- **Examinee town**: studying to pass a Wing's exam.
- **Outskirts-born**: abandoned child or settlement survivor (rough, rare, unusual in the City).

Origin sets accent, fashion (see quarters in 01), wealth and local Taboos.

## 3. Affiliation
Almost everyone belongs to something.

| Path | Notes |
|---|---|
| Fixer in an Office | Most common. Fixer License from Hana. Starts at Grade 9. Offices may be independent or Associate. |
| Association Fixer | Harder to enter (exams). Sections 6 (low) to 1 (high), four regional branches. Steady salary, cannot refuse jobs. |
| Taboo Hunter | Fixer hired by a Wing to chase violators, even across Districts with the other Wing's approval. |
| Butler | Long contract to a family or estate. Often retired Fixers. Contracts can be inherited and never really end. |
| Wing employee | Feather with a work visa and an office job or security role. |
| Syndicate member | Unlicensed. One of the Five Fingers, a subsidiary, or a small gang. |
| Rat | Lowest gangster. Too weak or unknown for a real Syndicate. Close-knit packs without hierarchy. |
| Workshop / Tailor | Makes weapons or special clothing. Needs approvals and patents. |
| Whaler | Great Lake sailor-Fixer (U Corp). |
| Ex-soldier | Smoke War veteran. Often poor and shunned, especially from fallen Wings. |

Fixer rules to remember:
- Fixers report every job to Hana. Hana grades Fixers and Offices from those reports, so grade does not always equal strength.
- Grades go 9 (new) to 1. Above Grade 1 are the Colors, chosen by Hana and not allowed as starting characters.
- An unlicensed person who does Fixer-type work is, in practice, a Syndicate.
- Associate Fixers may not work with Syndicates.

## 4. Body
Almost every Fixer has augmentation; doing Fixer work without it is considered nearly impossible. The brain is never replaced.

| Option | Notes |
|---|---|
| Natural | Cheap, fragile. Fine for civilians or hidden skill. |
| Augmentation | Strengthens muscles, heart, reflexes. Lets the body keep up with training; it does not give skill. |
| Tattoo augmentation | Cheap and common now (old patent expired). Hard skin, extra strength. |
| Partial prosthetic | Replaced limbs or organs. Cheap ones lose sensation. |
| Full-body prosthetic | Irreversible. Needs fuel/electricity, brain fluid and maintenance. Cheap bodies lose senses; top models manage emotion. Old square-box designs are outdated. |

Prosthetics make people careless about pain and death. Roleplay that.

## 5. Gear
- Fixers wear special fabric (Tailor-made clothes) instead of plate armor. Quality fabric protects like augmentation.
- Weapons come from Workshops. Melee and close combat are the norm.
- Guns are restricted and very expensive (see 04). Do not give a starting character a pile of firearms.
- Useful extras: dimensional case, registered weapon, Fixer license, Nest visa.
- Relics, Singularity-grade gear and E.G.O. are not starting equipment.

## 6. Grade and power
| Grade | Feel |
|---|---|
| 9-7 | Street level, newcomers |
| 6-4 | Professional, reliable |
| 3-2 | Elite, notable in a District |
| 1 | Top of the system |
| Color | Beyond grading (NPC only) |

New characters start at Grade 9 unless the player gives a story reason for more. Even Grade 9 characters can survive if smart.

## 7. Who they are
Ask the player:
1. What do they want that they cannot easily get?
2. Who or what do they belong to, and what does that cost them?
3. Who did they lose, or fear losing?
4. What is their price?
5. What do they hide?
6. How do they cope with the City's apathy?

## 8. Hard limits for characters
- No resurrected people (except through life insurance revival, which is brain-only and allowed).
- No human-like robots or sapient AI.
- No clone of a living person staying longer than 7 days.
- No sapient non-human living in the City.
- No Colors, Relics, E.G.O., Distortion, Shin/Mang, or owned Singularity at the start.
- Backstreets people should not start with Nest permits unless the story justifies it.
