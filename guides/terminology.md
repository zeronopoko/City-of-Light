# Terminology

Project Moon often reuses ordinary words for something specific. Use these meanings, not the real-world ones.

## Places and structure
- **The City**: the whole walled urban world. Not one city, a continent-like mass of 26 Districts.
- **District**: a territory run by one Wing, split into a Nest and Backstreets.
- **Nest**: the Wing-managed, safe, wealthy part of a District.
- **Backstreets**: the slum part, ruled by Syndicates and Offices.
- **Outskirts**: wasteland outside the City. Not a suburb.
- **Ruins**: deep underground area with monsters and Relics.
- **Great Lake**: the sea in the south. Divided into "Lakes" with laws.
- **Wing** (also "Wing of the World"): one of the 26 top corporations. Swearing "by the Wings" or "Wing-forsaken" is common.
- **Feather**: a Wing employee or Nest resident.
- **Corp**: short for a Wing: "K Corp", "W Corp". The letter is the District number.
- **Head / Eye / Claw**: the central authority and its two arms (surveillance, executions).
- **Arbiter / Beholder / Claw**: the Head's agents. Arbiters lead Claws (the killers). Beholders are believed to gather intelligence for the Eye, but this is unconfirmed.
- **Impurity** (Impuritas Civitatis): the top threat level, assigned only by the Head.
- **Singularity**: a Wing's unique patented technology that breaks physics.
- **Migration permit**: very expensive document to live in a Nest.
- **Visa**: temporary pass into a Nest. Work visas are easier.

## People and groups
- **Fixer**: licensed mercenary / handyman. Not a repairman. Graded 9 to 1.
- **Office**: a Fixer business (small or mid). **Associate Office** works directly under an Association.
- **Association**: large Fixer organization named after a number (Hana, Zwei, Tres, ...). Each has Branches (N/S/E/W) and Sections (6 to 1).
- **Color**: Grade 1 Fixer promoted beyond grading by Hana. Given a color name (Blue Reverberation, Red Mist...). Titles can be passed on after death.
- **Taboo Hunter**: Fixer hired by a Wing to chase violators.
- **Butler**: Fixer on a long-term household/estate contract.
- **Whaler**: Fixer who hunts Whales and Mermaids on the Great Lake.
- **Tailor**: makes special armor-like clothing.
- **Workshop / Meister**: weapon and gear makers. Meister is a skilled maker.
- **Syndicate**: any organized group without Fixer licenses. Gangs, cults, mafia, yakuza-like clans. Not a trade union.
- **The Five Fingers** (also "the hand of the Backstreets"): Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky. The largest Syndicates, rivals to Wings.
- **Rats**: not literal rats. Poor Backstreets thugs in small leaderless packs, too weak or unknown to count as a real Syndicate. They prey on weak people and may harvest organs. Many dream of joining a real Syndicate, but real members look down on them. Rats are close to each other because they have no hierarchy.
- **Stray Dogs, Dead Rabbits, Axe Gang, Smiling Faces**: real named Syndicates, not animals.
- **Sweepers**: not janitors. Humans turned into liquid inside metal suits, who clean the Backstreets at night and convert people into fuel.
- **Bloodfiend**: human vampire-like being. Kindred and Bloodbag are lower forms.
- **Peccatulum** (pl. Peccatula): failed Distortion, a lesser creature.
- **Abnormality**: monster born from a human mind (Lobotomy Corporation). **Distortion**: monster form from emotional collapse. **E.G.O.**: weapon/armor from controlled emotion.
- **Sinner**: member of Limbus Company bound to the Executive Manager (Dante).

## Syndicate ranks (examples)
- **Thumb**: Capo dei capi (boss), Sottocapo, Capo, Soldato.
- **Index**: Proselyte, Messenger, Proxy, Weaver (hidden). Orders are Prescripts.
- **Middle**: Great Sister/Brother, Big Brother/Sister, Little Brother/Sister. Revenge is recorded in the Book of Vengeance.
- **Ring**: Maestro, Docent, Student. Ring members make art; protectees must submit art.
- **Pinky**: members hold star titles (e.g. Tiangang Star). Hidden within other groups.

## Money and law
- **Ahn**: currency.
- **Taboo**: law. City Taboo (the Head) vs Nest Taboo (a Wing).
- **Night in the Backstreets**: roughly 80-minute lawless period each night.
- **Prescript**: an order from the Index, treated like a god's command.
- **Time Tax**: T Corp's tax paid in hours.
- **Office raid**: a Syndicate attacking a Fixer Office to gain fame.

## Threat levels
Urban Myth, Urban Legend, Urban Plague, Urban Nightmare, Star of the City, Impurity. Graded by how much people will pay to remove the threat.

## Tech terms
- **Augmentation**: strengthening the body.
- **Prosthetic**: replacement body part.
- **Dimensional case/container**: bag or case that stores a lot.
- **Corridor / Backdoor**: connecting doors across places.
- **Concept incinerator**: erases an invention from the world.
- **Monolith**: black stele that pushes Distortion.
- **Golden Bough**: leftover Lobotomy Corporation energy tech.
- **Enkephalin / Cogito**: energy / mind-material of Lobotomy Corporation.
- **Relic**: rare Ruins artifact (extremely valuable).
- **Shin / Mang**: rare inner-power techniques: Shin boosts the body; Mang is a glowing ring on a weapon.
- **WARP**: W Corp's teleport trains.

## Style notes
- Nicknames and titles ("the Red Gaze", "the Black Silence") are Color titles, not insults.
- "Family" in a Syndicate can mean strict hierarchy and obedience, or real loyalty. It varies by group.
- People swear on Wings, not gods.
